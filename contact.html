Opportunities

Join the DEAR Lab

We are always looking for passionate and curious minds to join our team. If you are interested in RNA biology, cancer research, mechanobiology, or quantum sensing, we would love to hear from you.

PhD Positions

Doctoral opportunities are available for enthusiastic candidates passionate about RNA biology, mechanotransduction, and disease modeling. Candidates holding national fellowships (CSIR/UGC-NET, DBT-JRF, ICMR-JRF, DST-INSPIRE) or interested in NUCSER PhD programs are encouraged to get in touch.

Research Internships

We offer research training opportunities for MSc students and undergraduates seeking hands-on experience in molecular biology, cancer cell mechanics, advanced imaging, and RNA biology. Short-term and dissertation projects are supported.

Postdoctoral Fellows

We welcome prospective postdoctoral researchers with a strong background in molecular biology, cellular mechanics, or quantum bio-sensing technologies. We actively support competitive applications for national fellowships (N-PDF, DBT-RA, ICMR-PDF).

Send your CV and research interests to arpita.ghosh@nitte.edu.in
