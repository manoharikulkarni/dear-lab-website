Our Vision

Understanding RNA to Engineer Disease Solutions

At the Disease Engineering and Advanced RNA Biology (DEAR) Lab, based at NUCSER, NITTE (Deemed to be University), Karnataka, we pioneer integrative approaches to unravel the complex regulatory architecture of non-coding RNAs and harness them for disease engineering.

Our core mission bridges fundamental discoveries in RNA biology—from regulatory long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) and microRNAs to higher-order G-quadruplex RNA conformations—with translational bioengineering. We explore cellular mechanobiology to determine how the physical microenvironment, substrate stiffness, and extracellular forces govern aggressive malignancies such as glioblastoma (GBM).

By coupling biomimetic 3D tumor models with pioneering quantum sensing technologies—utilizing nitrogen-vacancy (NV) diamond nanosensors—and novel small molecule and peptide nucleic acid (PNA) therapeutics, our laboratory aims to deliver targeted, transformative interventions for intractable diseases.

Research Areas

Pioneering Discovery in RNA & Disease Engineering

Investigating the regulatory mechanisms of non-coding RNAs, cell mechanics, quantum nanosensing, and innovative therapeutics.

lncRNA & miRNA Biology

Investigating G-quadruplex structures in long non-coding RNAs like MALAT1, delineating RNA-protein interactions with nucleolin and nucleophosmin, and developing targeted strategies against oncogenic miR-21.

Mechanosensing

Unraveling how the lncRNA NEAT1 and nuclear paraspeckles dynamically respond to extracellular mechanical cues across soft versus stiff matrix substrates in glioblastoma cellular systems.

Disease Modelling / GBM

Engineering physiologically relevant 2D and 3D tumoroid platforms to dissect glioblastoma progression, invasion dynamics, and the phenotypic consequences of NEAT1 and MALAT1 dysregulation.

RNA Sensing Technologies

Employing nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center quantum sensors and fluorescent nanodiamonds for non-invasive, nanoscale real-time tracking of intracellular RNA dynamics, temperature, and biochemical fluctuations.

Small Molecule Therapeutics

Designing conformation-specific peptidic aminosugars, self-assembled branched DNA nanostructures, and PNA-based inhibitors to modulate RNA structural motifs and synergistically silence oncogenic pathways.
