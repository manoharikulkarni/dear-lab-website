Dr. Arpita Ghosh

Assistant Professor Grade II | Principal Investigator, DEAR Lab

NUCSER, NITTE (Deemed to be University), Karnataka, India

Dr. Arpita Ghosh leads the Disease Engineering and Advanced RNA Biology (DEAR) Lab, focusing on understanding the roles of long non-coding RNAs and microRNAs in cancer biology, with a special emphasis on glioblastoma. Her research integrates mechanobiology, quantum sensing technologies, and small molecule therapeutics to develop novel approaches for disease understanding and treatment. With expertise spanning G-quadruplex RNA structures, miRNA biogenesis modulation, and 3D tumor modeling, her lab bridges fundamental RNA biology with translational disease engineering.
