About the PI

Dr. Arpita Ghosh hails from Kolkata, the City of Joy, in eastern India. Her scientific journey has been driven by a deep curiosity about how molecular systems work and how fundamental discoveries can be translated into solutions for human disease.

She began her academic journey with a B.Tech.–M.Tech. dual degree in Biotechnology from KIIT University, Odisha, followed by a Ph.D. in Biological Sciences from CSIR–Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology (IGIB), New Delhi, where she discovered the fascinating world of RNA biology and its structural and functional possibilities. She subsequently pursued postdoctoral research in the Department of Chemical Engineering at IIT Bombay, first as an Institute Postdoctoral Fellow and later as an ANRF National Postdoctoral Fellow, working at the intersection of RNA biology, cancer, mechanobiology, and disease engineering.

Today, as an independent researcher and PI, she leads the Disease Engineering and Advanced RNA Biology (DEAR) Lab at NUCSER, NITTE, built around a central question:

Can we understand RNA deeply enough to engineer better solutions for cancer?

Her research explores long non-coding RNAs, microRNAs, RNA structures and higher-order RNA organization, and how these regulatory systems respond to the physical tumour microenvironment. Her work integrates RNA biology, mechanobiology, 3D tumour models, advanced sensing technologies, and therapeutic approaches to uncover mechanisms of cancer progression and therapy resistance.

Dr. Ghosh is an awardee of several prestigious national and international recognitions and has presented her research at scientific forums across different parts of the world.

Away from the laboratory, Arpita finds joy in music, dance, visual storytelling, and science communication. She loves telling stories — whether through a scientific figure, a research talk, a camera, or simply sharing the everyday journey of being a scientist. For her, creativity and science are not two separate worlds; both begin with curiosity, imagination, and the courage to ask questions.

And that spirit of curiosity is at the heart of DEAR Lab.
